# The end-to-end tier, part 2: the Reachy Mini embodiment

**Status:** Done

Implements [specs/e2e.md](../specs/e2e.md) for the Reachy Mini embodiment: the real app in the MuJoCo sim the bridge's harness runs, with the cable as the robot's mic and hand photos as props in its scene; scenarios 1–4 (startup, a spoken turn, hand signs, an emotion). Locally on macOS (the viewer sim, BlackHole) first; the robot's CI job is a later plan, like the computer's was.

## Steps

1. Write the config, the conftest additions, the stage and the scenarios.
2. Run them on the Mac against the viewer sim (`REACHY_MINI_E2E_SIM_VIEWER=1`), BlackHole as the robot's mic, the laptop's speakers as its speaker. Fix what the run reveals.

4. Gate, push, CI green (the computer job still green with the plugin loaded).

## What building it revealed

- **The person's voice must play from another process.** On the robot the bridge's GStreamer capture of BlackHole stalled the moment the person's voice, in the test's process, started playing: 202 chunks in the 2 s before, then 22 in the next 4, all silent, and no error. From a child process it was heard at once. The computer's capture had shown the same stall, which setting the cable to 16 kHz had only made rarer; with the voice in a child process, every spoken scenario passes at BlackHole's default 48 kHz, so the pin is gone and the run no longer changes the device's settings.
- **The model breaks the persona's one-body rule.** Asked for an emotion, Claude Haiku issued `play_emotion` and `say` in the same turn in 2 runs of 3, so they ran at once. It is the model's behaviour, not the product's wiring: the scenario records an overlap in the run's report instead of failing (`specs/e2e.md`, open question 3).
- **The app crashed on Linux when vision started.** In the robot's CI job the process died with a segmentation fault while MediaPipe was first imported, on the hand stage's worker thread, the bridge's GStreamer threads running. Importing MediaPipe at the session's start, in the conftest, made the four scenarios pass; importing it in `GestureInput.start`, on the main thread but after the body was entered, crashed again (an abort, in matplotlib's FreeType extension, which MediaPipe pulls in). So the trigger is a native-library clash with the GStreamer media, not the thread: `build_system` imports MediaPipe before entering the body, where the real app needs it too.
- **The plugin leaves the computer alone.** Registered for the whole tier, it spawns no sim for the computer's scenarios; both files run in one invocation.

## Runs

On the Mac (2026-11-10), viewer sim, BlackHole 2ch as the cable at 48 kHz, the laptop's speakers as the robot's:

- The whole tier, both embodiments, passes 7/7, twice in a row, in about 2 min 10 s per run; the robot's four scenarios take about 85 s of it.
- The spoken scenarios pass twice more at 48 kHz with the pin removed.

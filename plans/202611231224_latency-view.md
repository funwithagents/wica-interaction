# The latency view

**Status:** Done

Implements `specs/latency-view.md`: the app collects the stamps its libraries publish, joins them per reaction, and the page shows a glance line and a live timeline on the main surface, and a summary, a table and a waterfall behind the debug toggle. It leaves out what the spec defers: the robot's output latency, the capture delay, the end-to-end report.

## Steps

1. **The siblings and the SDK.** `uv lock --upgrade-package` for each of the four, add the two dependencies, `uv sync --dev`. Run the whole verification before anything else: `say` and `say_stream` of `tts-engine` now return a value, and `vision-modules` stamps on another clock function. Fix what this alone breaks.
2. **The records and the pure parts** (`latency.py`): the data types, `wall_clock`, the nearest-rank statistics with their counting rule. Tested on hand-built records.
3. **The collector on spans built by hand.** A span processor that keeps live spans by id (bounded), finds a reaction's head by walking parents and a Command's steps by walking children, and reads a running span's events. Tests build each chain of the spec with a local `TracerProvider` and explicit times: speech with and without an end of speech, a dictation closed by the silence timer, a hand sign, an arrival, typed text, a follow-up of a Command and of a `<next>` note, a reaction that speaks and acts, a declared event that never came, each flag.
4. **`install_tracing`.** One provider per process, one forwarding processor, the collector of the newest system as its target; the exporter from `WICA_INTERACTION_TRACES`, with its two banner lines. A session-scoped fixture installs it for the tests.
5. **The collector on a real agent.** `build_system` builds it before `Wica.init` and subscribes it to the agent's events; `wire` hands it the effects. A test over `FakeEmbodiment` and WICA's fake model: a typed turn yields one record with the middle steps and a total ending at the fake voice's declared event.
6. **The embodiments' `effects()`.** The protocol member, the fake, the computer (both players, the stream's latency exposed by the app's player), the robot. Tests assert each declaration names a constant its library exports, so a rename there is a failure here.
7. **The perception span.** The helper; `HoldFilter` keeping the candidate's first-frame stamps (its timing logic unchanged, on `time.monotonic()`); `GestureInput` opening the span for a delivered sign; `UsersInput` around a `user_event`. Tests: the span is a root, starts at the origin, has the steps of the spec, and a reaction woken by it is recorded as its kind.
8. **Speech.** `AsrBridge` reports each segment's speech stamps; a test with the engine's fake module and a scripted VAD yields a `speech` record whose origin is the end of speech, and an `origin unknown` one without a VAD.
9. **The markup** (`latency_view.py`): the glance line, the timeline, the table rows, the waterfall, the summary. Tests assert on what a reader sees: a step's bar starts and ends at the right fraction of the axis, a wait and a not-measured step carry their class, a label with markup in it is escaped, an empty window renders the same markup twice.
10. **The page.** The components, the timer that sends nothing when unchanged, the row selection, the reset button, the debug row; the Vision panel's readout from `status()`.
11. **A run on each body**, checked by eye (below).
12. **The specs, the map, the docs**, and the statuses: `specs/latency-view.md` and this plan, in both indexes.

## Verification

`uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format .`, `uv run pyright`, `uv run pytest`.

By eye, on the computer and on the robot in the sim:

- a spoken turn: the `You` bar ends where the endpointing wait starts, the total ends where the `Voice` row turns solid, and the waterfall's two signal rows agree with its bars;
- a hand sign: the `Seen` row shows the hold as a wait of `vision.hold_s`;
- an emotion on the robot: the `Actions` bar starts at the motion, after the Command's start;
- speaking while the agent is busy: a dropped mark on the `Agent` row and the flag on the row;
- the statistics leave out the first reaction and the follow-ups, and the reset button empties them;
- `WICA_INTERACTION_TRACES=console` prints the spans of one turn as one trace from `asr_engine.segment` down to the voice.

# CI: the end-to-end tier's robot job

**Status:** In progress

Implements [specs/ci.md](../specs/ci.md) ("The workflow") for the end-to-end tier's Reachy Mini scenarios ([specs/e2e.md](../specs/e2e.md)): one Linux job running them against the headless sim the bridge's harness spawns, its camera rendered through EGL, over the same PulseAudio cable as the computer's job. Leaves out the nightly production run.

## Scope

- `.github/workflows/ci.yml` — the `e2e-reachy` job.
- `specs/ci.md` — the job, its devices, its required capabilities.
- `plans/_index.md` — this plan's row.

## Steps

1. Write the job from the computer's and the bridge's own `e2e-sim`: the app's packages plus PulseAudio with its ALSA and GStreamer plugins, the daemon's ICE library and Mesa's EGL; upstream's pinned webrtc plugin action; the cable with PulseAudio descriptions equal to the ALSA names, the mic and the speaker as defaults; `REACHY_MINI_E2E_REQUIRED_CAPS`; the scenarios with live warnings; the evidence on failure.
2. Push and read the job's log: the devices, the sim's probed capabilities, the daemon's audio report, then the scenarios. Fix what Linux reveals.
3. Mark this plan `Done` and `specs/ci.md` `Implemented`, here and in both indexes.

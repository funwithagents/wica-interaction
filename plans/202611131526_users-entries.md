# Users entries: one per concept, the bridge's word

**Status:** Todo

Implements [specs/reachy-embodiment.md](../specs/reachy-embodiment.md) ("Users: `UsersInput`") and the matching changes in [specs/app.md](../specs/app.md) ("World, Commands, persona") and [specs/e2e.md](../specs/e2e.md) (scenarios 4 and 5). It follows a first session on the robot, after [202611131434_people-awareness.md](202611131434_people-awareness.md). The robot's two entries (`person_event`, `people_in_view`) become four, one per concept, named with the bridge's word, *user*:

- `user_event`: arrived, left; wakes the agent.
- `users_in_view`: standing.
- `personal_space`: a state that wakes the agent when who is inside changes, entering and stepping out alike, with no cooldown.
- `tracked_user`: standing.

`start_tracking(person)` becomes `start_tracking(user)`. Nothing else in the design changes.
